Flagship

AI Visibility Audit

Starting at $8,500

What it is: A comprehensive audit of your brand's AI presence, scored against our proprietary 4 Pillars framework:

  • Accuracy — Does AI describe your products, claims, and credentials correctly?
  • Authority — Is AI citing real, credentialed sources, or filling gaps with guesswork?
  • Consistency — Does the story AI tells match across platforms, or contradict itself?
  • Credibility Signals — Are the structural signals (schema, entity data, verified profiles) in place for AI to get it right going forward?

Every finding is run through a compliance filter built for your industry — flagging anything that reads like an FDA claims issue, a HIPAA disclosure problem, or an SEC material-statement risk — and scored on a four-level flag scale (Critical / High / Moderate / Note) so you know what needs legal review versus what's an optimization opportunity.

What you get

  • Full platform-by-platform findings report
  • Competitive comparison
  • Compliance risk flags, prioritized by severity
  • A prioritized remediation roadmap

Who it's for: Companies who've had a Snapshot (or already suspect a problem) and need the full picture plus a plan — typically compliance officers, CMOs, legal teams, and founders in regulated categories.

Ongoing

AI Trust Governance Program

Monthly retainer

What it is: AI outputs drift. New model versions, new competitor content, and new claims in the wild all change what AI says about you over time — an Audit is a snapshot in time, not a permanent fix. The Governance Program keeps your AI visibility accurate and compliant on an ongoing basis.

What's included

  • Recurring re-audits on a set cadence (we use a 70/30 monitoring split — 70% tracking known risk areas, 30% scanning for new ones)
  • Priority alerts when AI outputs shift in a way that raises compliance risk
  • Hands-on implementation work — schema markup, entity consolidation, credential/source building — to correct what the Audit found
  • Direct access for compliance and legal teams when a flagged item needs their review

Who it's for: Companies who've completed an Audit and want AI accuracy maintained as part of their ongoing compliance posture, not treated as a one-time project.

A Note on Legal Counsel

Gold Light Digital audits and advises on AI visibility and the compliance patterns we see in AI-generated content — we are not a law firm, and nothing in an Audit or Governance engagement constitutes legal advice. When a finding touches on a specific regulatory determination (an off-label promotion question, a disclosure requirement, a PHI exposure concern), we'll flag it clearly and recommend you loop in your legal counsel before acting.
