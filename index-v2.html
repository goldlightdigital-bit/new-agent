How It Works

Inside the AI Visibility & Accuracy Audit

A clear, five-step methodology — the same one behind every Audit and every Snapshot.

  1. 01

    Scope

    We define your brand, treatments or products, approved claims, and the real questions your patients ask.

  2. 02

    Query run

    I run those questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot, and capture exactly what each says about you.

  3. 03

    4 Pillars scoring

    Every answer is scored on Accuracy, Authority, Consistency, and Credibility Signals for a clear baseline score.

  4. 04

    Claims-accuracy flags

    I flag where AI drifts from your approved claims — so you know what to surface for legal counsel. A list, never a legal opinion.

  5. 05

    Findings & roadmap

    You get a concise written report and a prioritized 30/60/90-day roadmap you can act on right away.

The Framework

The 4 Pillars I score against.

Accuracy

Does what AI says match your approved claims and current offerings?

Authority

Is your own content the cited source — or is someone else speaking for you?

Consistency

Do the answers hold together across platforms, pages, and over time?

Credibility Signals

Do the trust markers AI looks for — reviews, citations, structured data — actually point to you?
