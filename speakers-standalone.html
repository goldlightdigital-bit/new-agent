The Shift

The New Front Door

Google used to be where people found you. Now it's ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.

Someone asks "who's the best speaker on leadership" or "who should I hire as a business coach" — and AI gives them a confident, specific answer. No scrolling. No comparing five tabs. They just act on it.

If AI isn't recommending you, you're invisible in a conversation you didn't even know was happening.

The Problem

Ranking #1 on Google doesn't mean anything here.

AI platforms don't just repeat your top Google result. They synthesize from dozens of sources — some accurate, some outdated, some written by someone else entirely. The name that comes up when someone asks about your field might not be you. It might be a directory listing. An old bio. A competitor who happened to get cited more.

Most people have no idea what AI is currently saying about them — until they check.
