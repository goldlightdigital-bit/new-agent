How It Works

A clear path from unknown to defensible.

  1. 01

    Scope

    We define your brand entities, key claims, competitors, and the regulatory lines that matter — HIPAA, FDA, SEC, or state AI rules.

  2. 02

    Audit

    We test how the major AI platforms answer real buyer and regulator questions about you, and document every response with sources.

  3. 03

    Assess

    Each answer is scored for accuracy, sentiment, and risk against your own source of truth — no hand-waving, every flag traceable.

  4. 04

    Roadmap

    You get a prioritized, plain-English plan to correct inaccuracies and strengthen how AI represents you — plus a dashboard to track it.
