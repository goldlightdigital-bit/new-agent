The Compliance Layer

We don't call things "compliant." We flag them — by severity and by lens.

An agency shouldn't hand a regulated client a legal opinion. So we don't. Everything we produce for you is scored on a consistent severity scale and passed through three review lenses — and when something genuinely needs a determination, we recommend counsel rather than guess.

Four-level severity scale

  • Informational

    Noted for context. No action required.

  • Monitor

    Worth watching. Tracked run over run.

  • Action Needed

    A fix is recommended, with a clear next step.

  • Urgent / Escalate

    High-risk. Carries an explicit recommendation to involve legal counsel.

Three review lenses

Accuracy

Does the claim match your verified source of truth?

Authority

Is your own content the cited source — or is a third party speaking for you?

Consistency

Does the message hold together across platforms, pages, and time?

Every flag and decision is timestamped, forming a defensible record your compliance team can stand behind.
