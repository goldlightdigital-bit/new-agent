Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Gold Light Digital is committed to making its website accessible to as many people as possible, regardless of ability or technology. We view accessibility as part of the same standard of care we bring to our work with regulated brands.

Our Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive differences.

What We've Done

Steps we've taken on this website include:

  • Semantic HTML with clear landmarks and heading structure;
  • A "skip to content" link for keyboard and screen-reader users;
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, with visible focus indicators;
  • Color combinations chosen for readable contrast;
  • Descriptive labels on interactive controls, including the menu toggle;
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences, so animations respect your system settings; and
  • A responsive layout that adapts to different screen sizes and zoom levels.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content or third-party tools — such as our embedded scheduling provider — may not be fully accessible in every circumstance. We treat accessibility as ongoing work and continue to make improvements over time. If a third-party tool prevents you from completing a task, please contact us and we will help you directly.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback. If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, or need information in an alternative format, please email us at goldlightdigital@gmail.com. Let us know the page, the issue, and the assistive technology you were using, and we will do our best to respond promptly and provide the information or assistance you need.
