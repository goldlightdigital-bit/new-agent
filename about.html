About

I'm Kara Reagan. I make sure AI gets your brand right.

I spent nine years in digital marketing — SEO, paid media, content strategy — before a simple realization changed how I saw the work: people don't just ask Google about a brand anymore. They ask ChatGPT. They ask Perplexity. And most companies have no idea what those platforms are telling their customers, patients, or investors — let alone whether it's accurate.

For regulated industries, that blind spot isn't just a missed opportunity. It's exposure. An AI platform that implies an approval you don't have, misstates a claim, or fabricates a data-handling detail creates the same regulatory risk as if you'd published it yourself.

I founded Gold Light Digital to close that gap — auditing what AI says about brands in HealthTech, MedTech, Pharma, Medical Aesthetics, Nutraceuticals, and FinTech, flagging what's compliance-risky, and building the plan to fix it.

"AI is already answering for your brand. My job is to make sure those answers are accurate, attributable, and compliant." — Kara Reagan, Founder
