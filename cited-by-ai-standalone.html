The Problem

Buyers ask AI before they ever reach you.

Before a prospect visits your website, they're asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini who to trust, what's safe, and who's the best provider — and acting on the answer. Most brands have no idea what AI platforms are currently saying about them, or whether it's even accurate. By the time a wrong answer surfaces, it has already shaped a decision.
