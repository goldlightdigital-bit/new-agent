Why This Matters Now

AI platforms are becoming a distribution channel for claims about your brand — one you don't control and, until now, probably haven't audited.

When someone asks ChatGPT what a treatment does, whether a supplement is FDA-approved, or how a fintech product handles their data, the answer they get shapes trust and purchase decisions — before they ever reach your website or your compliance-reviewed materials.

For regulated industries, that's not just a marketing gap. An AI platform stating an unapproved use, implying a clearance a product doesn't have, or fabricating a data-handling claim can create the same exposure as if your own team had published it — HIPAA, FDA, and SEC frameworks don't distinguish between what your marketing team said and what a chatbot said about you.

Gold Light Digital exists to close that gap: we tell you exactly what AI is saying about your brand, flag what's compliance-risky, and build a plan to fix it.
