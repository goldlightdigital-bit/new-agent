For regulated industries, AI platforms are already answering questions about your brand, your products, and your claims — accurately or not. Gold Light Digital helps you find out, fix it, and keep it that way.

The Problem

AI doesn't know your compliance requirements.

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot are increasingly where people go to ask "is this safe," "what does this company do," or "who's the best provider for X." For regulated industries, that's a real risk: AI platforms synthesize information from dozens of sources, and they don't distinguish between an approved claim and an outdated one, an accurate description and a misleading one.

If AI is misrepresenting your company, your products, or your claims, you may not know until it's already a problem — a prospect who acted on bad information, a regulator who noticed before you did.

The Solution

See it. Fix it. Monitor it.

Gold Light Digital audits how AI platforms represent regulated companies — and builds the ongoing process to keep that representation accurate as AI platforms, and your business, continue to change.

Why This Is Different

This isn't internal AI governance.

Most AI governance programs look inward — how your company uses AI internally, what tools are approved, what data policies apply. That's necessary, but it's a different problem from this one.

This is about the outside view: what AI platforms say about you publicly, to anyone who asks. That's a gap most internal governance programs don't cover — and it's the one that's visible to your prospects, your regulators, and your competitors before you even know there's an issue.

Methodology

A framework built for regulated industries.

Every audit is scored against four pillars.

Accuracy

Is what AI says about you factually correct?

Authority

Does AI position you as a credible, established source?

Consistency

Does the story stay the same across platforms, or contradict itself?

Credibility Signals

Is AI citing real, current, trustworthy sources?

Findings are filtered through a compliance lens — regulatory category match, attribution risk, and plausibility — and flagged on a four-level scale, so your team can prioritize by severity rather than wade through an undifferentiated list.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What industries do you work with?

HealthTech, MedTech, Pharma, Medical Aesthetics and Dermatology, Nutraceuticals, and FinTech — and other regulated categories where AI misrepresentation carries real risk. We also work directly with founders in these spaces who want to know how AI represents them and their company before it becomes someone else's problem to explain.

How is this different from SEO or reputation management?

SEO gets you found on Google. Reputation management typically addresses reviews and press. This is specifically about what AI platforms say when someone asks them directly — a distinct system with its own sources, patterns, and risks.

Do you replace our internal compliance or legal team?

No. We audit and advise on AI visibility and representation; we don't practice law. Where something touches a regulatory or legal question directly, we'll flag it for your legal counsel.

How often should this be monitored?

AI platforms update frequently, and so does your business. Most clients move from a one-time audit into ongoing monitoring once they see how much can shift in a given month.
